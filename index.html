How it works

You send what you have. I handle the studio.

Day 1

Brief

Send your dieline, label art or a physical sample, plus where the images will run.

Days 2–3

Model

Accurate geometry and materials: glass, foil, matte paper, brushed aluminum.

Days 3–4

Light

Clay previews of camera angles and lighting for your sign-off before final render.

Day 5

Deliver

Final files on alpha, sized for your store, ads and print. The 3D scene stays ready for next time.
